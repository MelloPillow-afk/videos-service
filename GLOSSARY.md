# Pilgrims Videos

The shared library of videos managed by Pilgrims administrators and available to viewers after publication.

## Language

**Video library**:
The single collection of Pilgrims videos shared by all administrators.

**Administrator**:
A person authorized to manage every video in the video library, regardless of who created it.

**Viewer**:
A person browsing or watching ready, published videos; signing in is optional.

**Publication**:
The decision to make a ready video discoverable to viewers.
